# Phase 8 Decision

Optional Phase 8 was deliberately skipped for v1.

## Why
- The highest-value MVP work is already delivered by the rules-based tax/location/rebalancing engine.
- Scenario stress and read-only macro context would add reporting surface area, but not meaningfully improve the core on-demand audit decision for v1.
- Skipping this phase preserves the non-negotiable doctrine that recommendations remain fully deterministic and local.

## Guardrail
No macro or scenario engine exists in v1, so recommendation, urgency, and score remain unaffected by external commentary.
